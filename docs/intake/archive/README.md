# intake/archive/ — Preservation archive

Preservation zone for **fully-consumed** intake artifacts — functional designs, plans,
and rulings registers whose asks have been ratified/reconciled into ADRs, BACKLOG, or
process (per the 2026-07-18 deployment-status inventory). Distinct from `docs/archive/`,
the ADR-60 *pending-classification triage queue* (default-to-deletion after two reviews):
nothing here is on a deletion track.

Files land here when their intake's content is DEPLOYED with no open remainder. Each keeps
its original filename and its in-file `Deployment-Status` stamp. Seeded 2026-07-18 in the
consolidation archival pass.
